About Us

Trukai’s relationship with the people of Papua New Guinea spans over 45 years, beginning on February 17th 1970. The organization was initially incorporated as “Ricegrowers – Australia Pty. Limited”, and in its humble beginnings distributed only a single type of white and brown rice packed in polysac bags.

OUR PRODUCTS

The unique consistency and flavour of Trukai Rice

You may ask why Trukai Rice is Papua New Guinea’s favourite Rice. The unique consistency and flavour of Trukai Rice that appeals to all consumers is a result of many years of Research and Development based on Customer feedback. Trukai is committed to maintaining the quality and taste of all our brands through regular sensory and quality testing.

Trukai currently manufactures two brands of rice in the country; Trukai rice and Roots rice. Under the Trukai brand, we have Trukai medium grain, Jasmine and Natural Brown rice. Under Roots we have Roots medium grain rice and Roots long grain. We package and sell 18 formats of rice. These include medium grain, long grain, natural brown and jasmine blends.

Positive steps forward

Trukai believes it has a responsibility to contribute towards the growth of agriculture in Papua New Guinea. We have diversified our interests by establishing an agricultural division that focuses on maize cultivation and local rice farming.

Medium Grain Rice

Trukai Medium Grain Rice is Papua New Guinea’s favourite rice. The unique consistency and flavour of Medium Grain Rice that appeals to consumers is a result of many years of Research and Development based on customer feedback.

Trukai Medium Grain Rice is associated with health and strength, appropriately demonstrated by the company’s continued support of the sports of weightlifting, powerlifting and bodybuilding. Trukai Rice is available in 500g, 1kg, 5kg 10kg and 20kg pack sizes.

Jasmine Rice

A fragrant rice ideal for Asian style dishes such as fried rice, stir fries, red and green curries.

This rice appeals to the modern, adventurous Papua New Guinean consumer.

Natural Brown Rice

Natural Brown Trukai Rice is the healthiest of all Trukai rices. The bran coating on Natural Brown Trukai Rice is full of fibre and nutrition and is extremely good for growing children and those health conscious consumers. With a unique nutty flavour this rice is a complete meal on its own. Natural Brown Trukai Rice comes in 1kg, 10kg and 20kg pack sizes.

Roots Rice

Made from its own unique blend of rices, Roots Rice satisfies the needs of large families with many mouths to feed. Trukai Industries goes to extraordinary lengths to serve good quality rice at prices that ‘grass roots’ consumers can afford. Roots Rice is available in 500g, 1kg, 10kg sizes and 20kg catering packs.

Roots Long Grain

Roots Long Grain Rice is made from a unique blend of rice to provide additional support for families doing it tough . It provides families an affordable rice solution without the need to comprise quality.

Wantok Rice

Wantok: Tok Pisin word derived from “One” and “Talk”, denoting someone you share a language/culture with. Can apply to non-relatives as well. Wantok is made from a unique blend of grains for helping consumers that need basic sustenance to get through the day.

About Trukai

Trukai Industries is two thirds owned by SunRice Group. It is also proudly owned by the people of Papua New Guinea through the Pacific Balanced Fund (PBF) and its 22,000 national unit holders. Weathermen Capital Advisors is the Interim Trustee of the Pacific Balanced Fund.
