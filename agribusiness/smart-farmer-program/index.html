About Us

Trukai’s relationship with the people of Papua New Guinea spans over 45 years, beginning on February 17th 1970. The organization was initially incorporated as “Ricegrowers – Australia Pty. Limited”, and in its humble beginnings distributed only a single type of white and brown rice packed in polysac bags.

Smart Farmer Program

APPLICATIONS FOR ENROLMENT 

Are you ready to grow your skills and unlock new agricultural opportunities? The Trukai Smart Farmer Program, in partnership with Pacific Adventist University (Port Moresby), the PNG University of Technology (Lae), and the PNG University of Natural Resources and Environment (UNRE), is proud to invite applications for the Certificate in Irrigated Rice Farming Training—an exciting opportunity to build practical expertise and advance your rice farming skills while making a lasting impact in your community!

Eligibility

To be eligible to apply, applicant must:

  • Be between the ages of 25 to 50 years
  • Be physically fit
  • Provide a recent medical report from your local clinic
  • Have some experience in growing crops. (potato, kaukau etc.)
  • Have access to land close to a reliable water source.
  • Have a cropping area that’s easy to access by road.

How to Apply

Application forms can be obtained by;

  • Visiting your nearest Trukai Office
  • Email: TruFarmersTraining@trukai.com.pg

The completed form must be sent with a support letter from a village councilor or district administrator from your local area.

Send completed applications forms to TruFarmersTraining@trukai.com.pg or drop off at the nearest Trukai Office before:

  • University of Technology - 8th, June 2026 | Download Application
  • Pacific Adventist University - 15th, June 2026  | Download Application
  • PNG University of Natural Resources and Environment - 22nd, June 2026  | Download Application

For more information: Call 7373 8300 or Email: TruFarmersTraining@trukai.com.pg
