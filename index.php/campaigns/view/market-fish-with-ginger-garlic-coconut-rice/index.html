About Us

Trukai’s relationship with the people of Papua New Guinea spans over 45 years, beginning on February 17th 1970. The organization was initially incorporated as “Ricegrowers – Australia Pty. Limited”, and in its humble beginnings distributed only a single type of white and brown rice packed in polysac bags.

Market Fish with Ginger Garlic Coconut Rice

Ingredients:

Banana leaf Market fish  

  • Fish (market fish) mackerel, snapper, trevally cut into bite size pieces
  • 1g ginger (1 small knob) chopped  
  • 10pcs cherry tomato
  • 2 whole onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1 (bunch) spring onion
  • Salt (pinch)
  • 2g pepper cracked

Ginger Garlic Coconut Rice

  • 300g medium rice (washed and drained) 
  • 950g coconut milk 
  • 4g Salt (pinch) 
  • 4pcs Garlic (diced)
  • 4g Ginger (diced)

To cook the banana leaf wrapped fish:

  1. Place your fish pieces on a tray
  2. Season them with salt n pepper
  3. Place a handful of pieces onto banana leaves.
  4. Place 2 pcs of cherry tomatoes, sliced onions, ginger, spring onion on top of the fish
  5. Drizzle with oil and proceed to wrap the banana leaf parcels
  6. Once wrapped, place them into a pot of boiling water and cook for 5 - 10 minutes (depending on the size of the the fish pieces)

To cook the rice:

  1. Take a pot and place it on the heat. Add oil and begin to cook the garlic ginger and salt.
  2.  Once sautéed off add rice and begin to sauté
  3. Add the coconut milk to the rice, place the lid on and begin to simmer
  4. This will take roughly 20 to 25 mins

Serve up your Ginger Garlic Coconut Rice with the Banana Leaf Market Fish, enjoy!

About Trukai

Trukai Industries is two thirds owned by SunRice Group. It is also proudly owned by the people of Papua New Guinea through the Pacific Balanced Fund (PBF) and its 22,000 national unit holders. Weathermen Capital Advisors is the Interim Trustee of the Pacific Balanced Fund.
