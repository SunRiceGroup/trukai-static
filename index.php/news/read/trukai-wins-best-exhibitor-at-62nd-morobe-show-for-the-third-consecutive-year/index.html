About Us

Trukai’s relationship with the people of Papua New Guinea spans over 45 years, beginning on February 17th 1970. The organization was initially incorporated as “Ricegrowers – Australia Pty. Limited”, and in its humble beginnings distributed only a single type of white and brown rice packed in polysac bags.

Trukai wins Best Exhibitor at 62nd Morobe Show for the third consecutive year

Trukai Industries has been recognized as the Best Exhibitor at the 62nd Morobe Show, a testament to the company's commitment to excellence and community development.

The Morobe Show, during the weekend in Lae, Morobe Province, coincided with Papua New Guinea's 50th Independence anniversary celebrations under the theme "50 Years Forward."

Head of Trukai Industries Andrew Grace said, "We are incredibly proud of this achievement, and I want to extend my sincere thanks to the Morobe Show Committee for their outstanding

work in organizing such a significant event. This victory belongs to our hardworking staff, dedicated farmers, and the communities we serve."

The company's impressive display featured rice paddy demonstrations, milling processes, and a paddy-to-plate information center, displaying its dedication to innovation and sustainability in Papua New Guinea's agricultural sector.

In recognition of Trukai's contribution, William Rannie, Vice President of the Morobe Show Committee, praised the company's efforts.

"On behalf of the Morobe Show Committee, I want to thank Trukai Industries for their unwavering support to the show," said Mr. Rannie. "Their dedication is evident in the quality of their display, which has consistently raised the bar and helped us provide an exceptional experience for all visitors. Trukai's effort to showcase the best of agriculture and community spirit is truly commendable."

Trukai Industries' continued success at the Morobe Show highlights its reputation as a trusted household brand and strong supporter of community development, fostering innovation, sustainability, and pride in Papua New Guinea's rich agricultural traditions.

About Trukai

Trukai Industries is two thirds owned by SunRice Group. It is also proudly owned by the people of Papua New Guinea through the Pacific Balanced Fund (PBF) and its 22,000 national unit holders. Weathermen Capital Advisors is the Interim Trustee of the Pacific Balanced Fund.
