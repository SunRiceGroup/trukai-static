About Us

Trukai’s relationship with the people of Papua New Guinea spans over 45 years, beginning on February 17th 1970. The organization was initially incorporated as “Ricegrowers – Australia Pty. Limited”, and in its humble beginnings distributed only a single type of white and brown rice packed in polysac bags.

Seafood Paella with Trukai Medium Grain Rice 

  • Serves: 4  
  • Prep Time: 15 mins  
  • Cook Time: 30 mins  

Ingredients:

  •  2 cups Trukai Medium Grain Rice
  •  300g prawns, peeled and deveined  
  •  200g squid rings 
  • 300g mussels 
  •  1 red bell pepper, sliced  
  •  1 yellow bell pepper, sliced  
  •  1 small onion, diced  
  •  3 cloves garlic, minced  
  •  1 tsp paprika  
  •  1 tsp turmeric or a pinch of saﬀron (optional)  
  •  Salt and pepper to taste  
  •  1 lemon, cut into wedges  
  •  Fresh coriander for garnish  
  •  3 tbsp cooking oil 
  •  4 cups chicken or seafood stock

---

Instructions to Cook:

1. Sauté aromatics: 

   In a large pan or paella pan, heat oil over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, sauté until soft.

2. Add peppers & spices:  

   Add bell peppers and cook for 2–3 minutes. Stir in paprika, turmeric (if using), salt, and pepper.

3. Add rice:  

   Stir in Trukai Medium Grain Rice and mix well to coat with spices and oil.

4. Pour in stock: 

   Add the stock and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and let simmer for 10 minutes.

5. Add seafood:

   Gently place prawns, squid, and mussels on top of the rice. Cover again and cook for another
10–15 minutes, or until rice is cooked and seafood is done.

6. Rest & garnish: 

   Turn oﬀ the heat and let the paella sit for 5 minutes. Garnish with fresh coriander and lemon
wedges.

Enjoy your delicious, island-style seafood paella with Trukai Rice! �

About Trukai
